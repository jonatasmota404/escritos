When distributed services depend on each other synchronously, a single point of slowness can cause systemic collapse. The **Circuit Breaker** pattern exists to stop calls that are doomed to fail before they consume all of the application's resources.

### The State Cycle

The circuit operates in three fundamental states:

<Mermaid chart={`graph LR
Closed[Closed: Normal Flow] -->|Consecutive failures exceed threshold| Open[Open: Fast Rejection]
Open -->|Time window expires| HalfOpen[Half-Open: Test Request]
HalfOpen -->|Success| Closed
HalfOpen -->|Failure| Open`} />

### Why Use It in Production?

- **Fail-Fast:** Avoids leaving connections hanging while waiting on a 30-second timeout.
- **Self-Recovery:** Lets the unstable external service recover without being hit by a continuous flood of traffic.
- **Graceful Degradation:** Makes it easier to return cached data or a controlled fallback to the user.
