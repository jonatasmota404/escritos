Conventional logs show *what* went wrong at a fixed point, but not *how long* each step of a request took as it moved through the ecosystem.

### How a Trace Works

A **Trace** is made up of a tree of **Spans**, where each span represents a unit of work with a marked start and end:

<Mermaid chart={`graph TD
Root[HTTP POST /orders (Total: 120ms)] --> Auth[Auth Middleware (10ms)]
Root --> DB[Query Postgres (85ms)]
Root --> Queue[Publish Broker Event (15ms)]`} />

### What to Watch For When Instrumenting

- **Context Propagation:** Make sure the tracing header is passed along through every internal call.
- **Overhead:** Instrument critical I/O calls without creating microscopic spans for purely in-memory operations.
