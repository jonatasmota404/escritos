In CI/CD pipelines, the size of the final image directly affects deploy time, node startup speed, and network traffic costs.

### The Multi-Stage Principle

We separate the build environment (which requires compilers, type-checking tools, and heavy development dependencies) from the minimal final runtime environment:

### Practical Benefits

1. **Security:** The production container doesn't carry build tools that could be exploited after a breach.
2. **Layer Caching:** Dependencies that change less frequently sit in isolated layers, speeding up CI caching.
3. **Lightness:** Final images based on minimal distros (like Alpine or distroless) start up almost instantly.
